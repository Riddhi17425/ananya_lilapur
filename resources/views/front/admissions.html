Preprimary

A joyful start to lifelong learning.

The early years are when children discover how much they love to learn. Our Preprimary programme, from Play School to Sr. KG, gives young learners a warm, safe and joyful space to explore, play, ask questions and grow in confidence every day.

  • Play-based early learning

    Hands-on activities, stories and games that make learning feel natural and fun.

  • Strong foundations

    Early language, phonics, numbers and thinking skills, built step by step.

  • Social and emotional growth

    Learning to share, take turns, make friends and understand feelings.

  • Ready for school

    Independence, routines and listening skills for a confident move to Class 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which board does Ananya follow?

Ananya follows the CBSE curriculum. The affiliation process takes time, and the affiliation number will be shared once it is officially allotted.

What is the class size?

Each class has 35 students, in a classroom of approximately 650 sq. ft.

Is there an entrance test?

For Play School to Class 2, we hold a friendly interaction with no written test. For higher classes, a simple assessment helps us place the child in the right class.

Do you provide school transport?

Yes, we run school buses on planned routes. Please see the Transport section for details and the enrolment form.

Is there a cafeteria?

Yes, we offer a hygienic cafeteria.

What are the school timings?

Please contact our admissions desk for the timings for each stage.

How can I stay in touch with my child’s progress?

Through monthly parent club interactions, progress reports and our parent portal. You can also approach the school whenever you have a query.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Of course. Please book a campus visit and we will show you around.

Which sports and activities are available?

Please see the Beyond Academics page for the full list.
