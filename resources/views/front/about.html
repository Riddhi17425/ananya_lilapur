About Ananya International School

Ananya International School, Lilapur is a CBSE school that believes childhood deserves curiosity, courage and care. From a child’s very first day at Play School to the farewell at Class 8, we walk beside every learner and every family.

Our Legacy

A Journey of Learning. A Legacy of Growth.

Ananya’s story began with Dr. Geetaben Patel’s commitment to children and education. Today, the next generation carries that care forward, bringing fresh possibilities while staying close to the values that shaped us.

We see legacy not simply as something to preserve, but as wisdom to build upon. What generations have taught us gives us perspective; what the future asks of us calls for fresh thinking. At Ananya, these two ideas come together in the way we approach learning, relationships and the growth of every child.

Vision, Mission & Values

What We Stand For

Our Vision

To nurture curious, capable and caring young people who keep growing beyond what they are today.

We want every child to be known by name, encouraged to ask questions and supported to become a confident, responsible member of their family, their community and the wider world.

Our Mission

  • To make learning purposeful by connecting concepts to real experiences
  • To welcome questions and help children think, investigate and reason
  • To give every child thoughtful attention, whatever their abilities, interests and ways of learning
  • To build character and 21st-century skills alongside academic learning
  • To grow together with families as partners in every child’s journey

Our Core Values

  • The Child Comes First

  • Education with Purpose

  • Integrity in Every Action

  • Learning Beyond the Classroom

  • Growing Together

Every child is born curious!

Our Educational Philosophy

Why Ananya?

Parents have many good school options. Here is what makes Ananya different:

  1. 01

    Learning with purpose.

    Children connect concepts to experiences and discover how knowledge can be used.

  2. 02

    Questions are welcome.

    We encourage children to think, investigate and look for reasons rather than simply accept information.

  3. 03

    Every child matters.

    Different abilities, interests and ways of learning deserve thoughtful attention.

  4. 04

    Action-driven character development.

    Respect, empathy, responsibility and integrity are developed through everyday choices and interactions.

  5. 05

    Developing change makers.

    We nurture 21st-century skills alongside academic learning.

  6. 06

    Leadership you can meet.

    The Director and academic team are accessible to families.

We do not believe in a single measure of success. A child who loves science, one who dances, one who leads and one who quietly helps others are all successful. Our job is to help each of them find their peak.
