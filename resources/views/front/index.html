Ananya’s Educational Philosophy

Education That Builds the Whole Child

At Ananya, learning has a purpose. Children connect concepts to real experiences, questions are always welcome, and every child matters. Respect, empathy, responsibility and integrity grow through everyday choices, and 21st-century skills are nurtured alongside academic learning.

QUEST Pedagogy

Meet QUEST, Our Way of Teaching and Learning

QUEST
  • Q

    QuestionCuriosity sparks learning.

  • U

    UnderstandBuild meaning, not just memory.

  • E

    ExploreDiscover through experience.

  • S

    ShareExpress and reflect.

  • T

    TransformApply knowledge to the real world.

QUEST is the teaching-learning approach that shapes every classroom at Ananya.

In a QUEST classroom, children explore before they are told, question before they memorise, and apply what they learn to real situations. Teachers guide, challenge and encourage, while children take ownership of their learning.

What this means for your child

Learning is active, not passive

Concepts are understood, not just remembered

Every child is heard and encouraged to think independently

Progress is measured through understanding, skills and confidence, not marks alone
