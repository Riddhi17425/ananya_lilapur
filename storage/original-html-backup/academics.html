Learning That Goes Beyond the Textbook

Our academic programme follows the CBSE framework and brings it to life through the QUEST approach. The result is a classroom where children think, question, create and understand, and where strong results follow naturally.

Curriculum & CBSE Framework

A Strong CBSE Foundation

The CBSE framework provides the academic foundation for learning at Ananya. Our approach brings curriculum expectations together with meaningful classroom experiences, helping children understand concepts rather than rely only on memorisation. We aim to develop subject knowledge, language and mathematical proficiency, scientific thinking and the ability to apply learning in different contexts.

What our curriculum offers

  • A 5+3+3+4 learning structure, with a play-based Foundational Stage followed by Primary and Middle School
  • Languages: English, Hindi and Gujarati.
  • Core subjects: Mathematics, Science, Social Science and Computer Education
  • Integrated learning through projects, field visits and cross-subject activities
  • Strong emphasis on reading, writing, numeracy and communication

Why CBSE?

CBSE is recognised across India and is a preferred pathway for competitive exams, school transfers and higher education. Families moving between cities can be confident that their child’s learning will continue without a break.

QUEST Pedagogy

Teaching-Learning Methodologies

Learning that builds understanding.

Academic learning at Ananya is designed to help children build strong foundations, make connections and apply what they know. We combine the structure of the CBSE curriculum with teaching approaches that encourage inquiry, participation and independent thinking. As children progress through the school, they are supported in developing the knowledge, skills and confidence needed for increasingly complex learning.

QUEST: Five stages. One meaningful learning journey.

Q

Question

Curiosity sparks learning.

Every meaningful discovery begins with a question. Children meet stories, situations and real-life experiences that invite them to think, predict and connect new concepts with their own experiences.

Story-based learning · Inquiry-based learning

U

Understand

Build meaning, not just memory.

Children make sense of new ideas by connecting them with prior knowledge, discussing possibilities and identifying patterns, so they understand the “why” behind the “what”.

Visual learning · Concrete-to-abstract learning

E

Explore

Discover through experience.

Children investigate, experiment, create, observe and try different approaches to deepen their understanding.

Experiential learning · Learning by doing

S

Share

Express and reflect.

Children explain their thinking, listen to others and learn through discussion and collaboration. Sharing builds empathy, vocabulary and confidence in their own voice.

Discussion · Collaboration

T

Transform

Apply knowledge to the real world.

Learning moves beyond the lesson as children apply what they have understood, reflect on their progress and use their knowledge in new situations.

Project-based learning · Problem-based learning

Where the joy of learning begins.

The early years shape how children experience the world, build relationships and approach learning.

Foundational Stage · Play School to Class 2

Where Little Learners Take Their First Steps

At Ananya, the Foundational Stage focuses on language, early numeracy, movement, play, imagination and social-emotional development. Stories, conversations, games, music and hands-on activities help children make sense of the world around them. We give young learners opportunities to explore at their own pace, express themselves and develop the confidence to try something new.

  • Play-based and activity-based learning
  • Early language, number sense and motor skills
  • Storytelling, rhymes, music, movement and art
  • Sensory and outdoor play
  • Social and emotional learning: sharing, kindness and confidence

Primary School (Grades 3 to 5)

From discovering ideas to making connections.

The primary years are a time when curiosity grows into deeper understanding. Children strengthen their reading, writing and mathematical skills while exploring science, the environment, people and the world around them. Through discussions, activities, projects and collaborative tasks, they learn to connect ideas and express their thinking. Alongside academic progress, we encourage independence, responsibility, kindness and a willingness to learn from mistakes.

Middle School (Grades 6 to 8)

Thinking deeper. Looking wider.

As children grow, their questions become more complex and their perspectives begin to widen. Middle school learning encourages students to analyse information, consider different viewpoints, investigate problems and work with greater independence. Subject knowledge is strengthened through application, discussion, research and interdisciplinary connections. Students also begin to understand their interests, recognise their strengths and take greater ownership of their learning.

Assessment & Evaluation

Assessment @Ananya

Assessment at Ananya is intended to do more than measure performance. It helps teachers understand what children have learnt, where they need support and what the next step should be. Through appropriate assessments, classroom observations, assignments, projects and feedback, we aim to make learning progress visible.

  1. 01

    Observe.

    How the child participates, explains and approaches a task.

  2. 02

    Look at evidence.

    Class work, conversations, activities, projects and demonstrations.

  3. 03

    Give feedback.

    Identify what is going well and what the child can try next.

  4. 04

    Plan next steps.

    Use what teachers notice to guide further practice or challenge.

Children are encouraged to reflect on their work, recognise areas for improvement and understand that progress comes through effort, practice and guidance.
