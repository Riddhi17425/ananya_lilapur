Because Childhood Is Bigger Than a Classroom

At Ananya, activities are not extras. They are where children discover talent, build discipline, make friends and learn to lead. Every child gets time, space and coaching to explore.

Visual Arts

Imagine it. Create it. See it differently.

Art gives children the freedom to explore ideas, experiment with materials and express their individual perspectives. Drawing, painting, craft and other creative experiences encourage observation, imagination, patience and original thinking. The process matters as much as the finished work: trying, revising and discovering new possibilities are all part of learning.

Clubs & Activities

There is more than one way to discover a passion.

Clubs and activities give students opportunities to explore interests beyond their regular subjects. From creative pursuits and reading to science, technology, environmental awareness and collaborative projects, students can discover what excites them, learn alongside others and develop skills through shared experiences.

Life Skills & 21st-Century Skills

Capable minds. Considerate choices. Responsible action.

The world asks children to do more than know the right answers. It asks them to think critically, communicate clearly, work with others and respond thoughtfully to unfamiliar situations. At Ananya, we aim to nurture these capabilities alongside values that guide how children use them.

  • Critical thinking

  • Creativity

  • Communication

  • Collaboration

  • Digital responsibility

  • Empathy

  • Integrity

  • Resilience

  • Self-management

  • Social responsibility

Student Leadership

Learning to Lead, Learning to Serve

At Ananya, students are given real opportunities to speak, decide and lead. Our Student Council space is where student voices are heard, ideas are discussed and responsibility is shared, helping children grow into thoughtful, confident leaders.

  • Student council and house system
  • Class and club leadership roles
  • Community service and social-impact projects
  • Leadership workshops for middle-school students
