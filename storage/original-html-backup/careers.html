Careers

Build a future in education. Make a difference every day.

At Ananya, we believe the quality of a child’s learning experience is shaped by the people who guide it. We welcome educators and professionals who bring curiosity, care, commitment and a willingness to grow: people who see education not as the delivery of a syllabus, but as the thoughtful work of helping young minds develop. If you believe in creating meaningful learning experiences and contributing to a school that continues to evolve, we would like to hear from you.

Working at Ananya

A shared purpose. Many ways to make a difference.

Working at Ananya means contributing to a learning community where academic growth, relationships, responsibility and new ideas matter. Every role, whether in teaching, leadership or school operations, contributes to the experience we create for children and families.

Our Work Culture

A school takes shape through the people who care for its children.

We are looking for thoughtful educators and committed professionals who share our belief in meaningful learning and growing together.

Professional Growth & Development

Stay curious, keep learning.

At Ananya, educators are encouraged to stay curious, reflect on their practice and keep learning. Our teachers are trained and certified in QUEST pedagogy through capacity building, classroom implementation, observation and feedback, and continuous improvement.
